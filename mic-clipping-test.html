Audio Staging Rules

The Golden Gain Zones

  • -18 dBFS to -12 dBFS (Sweet Spot): Normal speaking voice should comfortably bounce in this green zone, leaving plenty of headroom.
  • -6 dBFS (Loud Peak): Excited speaking, laughs, or shouts should peak here without hitting the red ceiling.
  • 0 dBFS (Digital Brickwall): Any sound reaching 0 dBFS produces harsh, buzzing clipping distortion that cannot be repaired in software.
