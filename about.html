About InputTest.online

A free, browser-based hardware diagnostic platform — no downloads, no sign-ups, no data collection.

🎯 Our Mission

InputTest.online was created with a simple goal: give everyone a fast, reliable way to diagnose their hardware devices without installing any software. Whether you are a gamer checking your mouse click speed, a developer verifying keyboard inputs, or a professional testing microphone quality — InputTest has a tool for you.

We believe hardware testing should be free, instant, and private. Every test on our platform runs entirely in your browser using standard Web APIs — zero data ever leaves your device.

💡 Why We Built This

Many hardware testing tools require software downloads, account registrations, or send your data to remote servers. We built InputTest.online because we wanted a single, trusted place where anyone could instantly test 20+ devices — from CPS click speed to battery health — without privacy concerns or bloatware.
