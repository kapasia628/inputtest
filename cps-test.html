What is a CPS Test?

A CPS Test (Clicks Per Second Test) measures how many times you can click your mouse button in a given time period. It's a popular benchmark among gamers, especially in games like Minecraft PvP, where higher CPS gives a competitive advantage in combat.

Our free online CPS test offers multiple timer modes from 1 second to 60 seconds, letting you test your sustained clicking speed and endurance. Your personal best is automatically saved in your browser.

Clicking Techniques:

  • •
    Regular Clicking: Normal single-finger clicking. Averages 4-7 CPS.
  • •
    Jitter Clicking: Tensing your arm to vibrate the finger. Achieves 9-14 CPS.
  • •
    Butterfly Clicking: Alternating two fingers rapidly. Can exceed 15 CPS.
  • •
    Drag Clicking: Dragging finger across button. Not recommended for hardware health.
