How Fast Should You Type?

Typing LevelWPM RangeDescription
🐢 Beginner< 30 WPMHunt and peck typist, still learning
📝 Average40–60 WPMMost people type at this speed
✅ Good60–80 WPMAbove average, professional level
⚡ Fast80–100 WPMTouch typist, programmer or writer
🚀 Expert100+ WPMExceptional — top 1% of typists

The average typing speed for adults is 40-50 WPM. Trained touch typists can achieve 70-100+ WPM. The world typing speed record is an incredible 212 WPM by Barbara Blackburn. Regular practice can improve your speed significantly.

Tips to Type Faster:

  • ✅ Learn proper finger placement (home row)
  • ✅ Practice without looking at keyboard
  • ✅ Focus on accuracy first, speed follows
  • ✅ Practice 15-30 minutes daily
  • ✅ Use all 10 fingers (touch typing)

FAQ

60 WPM is considered good for most jobs. 80+ WPM is excellent. Professional data entry requires 60-80 WPM. Developers and writers often type 70-90 WPM.
WPM = (Total characters typed ÷ 5) ÷ minutes. Each "word" is standardized as 5 characters. Only correctly typed words count. This test measures net WPM (errors subtracted).
With daily 20-30 minute practice, most people improve by 10-15 WPM within 2-4 weeks. Going from 40 to 70 WPM takes about 2-3 months of consistent practice. Learning touch typing can double your speed over 3-6 months.
