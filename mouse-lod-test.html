📏 How to Run the Physical DVD Thickness Verification Test:

📀 Step 1: Place 1 CD / DVD

A standard optical disc is exactly 1.2mm thick. Place one disc flat on your mousepad and glide the mouse over it.

🔍 Step 2: Observe Tracking

If the cursor does NOT move, your mouse has Sub-1.2mm Low LOD (ideal for competitive FPS gaming).

⚠️ Step 3: Test 2 CDs (2.4mm)

If your mouse still tracks on top of 2 stacked CDs (>2.4mm), you have extreme high LOD causing crosshair drift.

< 1.0 mm — Esports Standard

PAW3950 / Focus Pro sensors. Absolute zero crosshair drift when picking up the mouse during 180° flick resets.

1.2 – 2.0 mm — Acceptable

Decent for casual gaming. Noticeable 5-15 pixel crosshair nudge when lifting quickly during arm swipes.

> 2.5 mm — High / Flawed LOD

Common on cheap office sensors. Mouse keeps tracking mid-air, destroying crosshair placement and micro-adjustments.

The Importance of Lift-Off Distance in Competitive Gaming

In games like Counter-Strike 2, Valorant, and Tom Clancy's Rainbow Six Siege, professional players utilize low sensitivity (such as 400 or 800 DPI with low in-game multipliers) for pinpoint headshot accuracy. At low sensitivity, turning 180 degrees requires sweeping your entire arm across a 45cm mousepad, rapidly lifting the mouse, and setting it down in the center.

Asymmetrical Cutoff: Lift-Off vs Landing Distance

Traditional mice use a single fixed focal threshold. When lifting, the sensor cuts off at 1.5mm, and when setting it down, it resumes tracking at 1.5mm. Modern esports mice feature Asymmetrical Cut-off:

  • Lift Distance: Set to a low 1.0mm or 0.7mm so tracking cuts off the instant your hand begins elevating.
  • Landing Distance: Configured to wait until the mouse is within 0.5mm of the surface before resuming tracking, eliminating accidental cursor jerks upon surface touchdown.

The "Tape Trick": Quick DIY Fix for High LOD

If you are stuck playing on a budget mouse with high lift-off distance (> 3mm), take a small piece of opaque black electrical tape and cover the bottom half of the clear sensor lens opening. This partially blocks the reflection angle of the infrared LED, lowering the focal plane and reducing the effective LOD down to ~1.2mm.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why is my cursor shaking when I lift my mouse?

Your mouse sensor has a high lift-off distance. While your mouse is mid-air, the optical sensor's LED continues illuminating microscopic texture variations on your desk, translating hand tilt into erratic crosshair displacement.

Can mouse skates (feet) alter LOD?

Yes! Installing thicker aftermarket PTFE or glass skates (e.g. 0.8mm instead of 0.6mm) raises the sensor further off the surface, effectively lowering the remaining usable tracking height.

How do I change mouse LOD in software?

In your mouse software (Razer Synapse, Logitech G HUB, Wooting, or Pulsar Fusion), open the Sensor / Calibration tab and look for "Lift-off Distance" or "Surface Tuning". Select "Low" or "1mm".

Does mousepad surface texture affect LOD?

Yes. Deep soft cloth pads (such as Artisan XSoft or SteelSeries QcK Heavy) allow the mouse to sink into the foam when pressed down, increasing the perceived lift travel compared to hard plastic or glass mousepads.
