What is Reaction Time?

Reaction time is the interval between a sensory stimulus (like a color change) and your motor response (clicking). This test measures visual reaction time — how quickly your eyes detect the green color and your brain sends a signal to your finger.

The average human visual reaction time is 200–250 milliseconds. Competitive gamers in FPS games like CS2, Valorant, and Apex Legends typically achieve 150–200ms, giving them a significant in-game advantage.

Tips to Improve Reaction Time:

  • •

  • •

  • •
    Exercise: Physical fitness improves neural processing
  • •
    Low monitor latency: High refresh rate monitors reduce input lag
