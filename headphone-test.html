Headphone Channel Separation & Spatial Acoustics

Stereo audio relies on two discrete audio channels: Left and Right. When mixing music or rendering 3D spatial footsteps in video games, audio engineers utilize Interaural Time Difference (ITD) and Interaural Level Difference (ILD). If a sound originates from your left, it reaches your left eardrum approximately 0.6 milliseconds before your right eardrum, with slightly higher high-frequency energy.

If your headphone drivers are wired with inverted polarity (one positive wire swapped with ground), the sound waves from both ears collide out-of-phase in your cranium, physically canceling lower frequencies and destroying your ability to pinpoint enemy locations in games.
