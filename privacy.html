Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to InputTest.online. We prioritize user privacy and safety above all else. This privacy policy describes how we collect, protect, and handle data when you browse our website and use our web-based hardware diagnostics tools.

🔒 2. 100% Client-Side Protection Disclosure

All our hardware checker modules (including the Keyboard Matrix Tester, click logs, and Clicks Per Second speed games) are calculated **entirely within your browser's local sandbox memory using client-side JavaScript APIs**. We do NOT store, capture, record, log, or transmit any inputs, key sequences, passwords, or hardware data to any external databases or server systems. Your typing sequences are 100% secure and invisible.

3. Personal Data Collection

Since InputTest.online operates completely client-side:

  • We do not require user registrations or account creations.
  • We do not record IP addresses associated with input sequences.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone configurations unless you choose to contact us directly via email.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

This site serves third-party advertisements via Google AdSense to fund site operations. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

5. Cookies and Logs

Like many websites, we utilize basic, standard technical cookies to save local configurations (such as storing your personal Clicks Per Second high scores). These cookies do not contain personally identifying information.

6. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Under California (CCPA) and European (GDPR) regulations, users have rights regarding data transparency and deletion. Because we **never gather or store personal details or hardware event streams on our servers**, we maintain a zero-log infrastructure, satisfying the highest thresholds of GDPR and CCPA standard guidelines automatically.
