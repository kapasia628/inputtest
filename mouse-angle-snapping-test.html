🎯 What is Mouse Angle Snapping (Sensor Prediction)?

Angle Snapping (often referred to as sensor prediction or axis correction) is an algorithmic post-processing filter embedded inside optical mouse firmware. When you guide your mouse across a mousepad, human hands naturally produce tiny, microscopic fluctuations (wobbles).

For office workers drawing horizontal borders in Microsoft Excel or dragging folders across a desktop, these wobbles can feel imperfect. To "help" the user, an angle-snapping algorithm monitors cursor vectors. If the movement angle is within approximately ±5° to ±15° of a straight horizontal or vertical trajectory, the sensor forcibly locks all output coordinates to a single axis ($dy = 0$ or $dx = 0$).

While convenient for spreadsheets, angle snapping is catastrophic for competitive FPS gamers. When attempting to land a pixel-perfect headshot in Valorant or track an airborne enemy in Apex Legends, the mouse will artificially refuse to register subtle micro-adjustments, causing your crosshair to "snap" onto a rigid horizontal plane instead of following your real physical aim.

🛠️ How to Disable Angle Snapping & Enable 1:1 Raw Aim

1. Check Driver Suite Settings

Open your mouse companion app (Razer Synapse, Logitech G HUB, SteelSeries Engine). Open Performance or Sensor calibration. Look for "Angle Snapping" or "Prediction" and verify it is set to OFF.

2. Uncheck "Enhance Pointer Precision"

In Windows, open Settings > Bluetooth & Devices > Mouse > Additional mouse settings. Switch to the Pointer Options tab. Ensure "Enhance pointer precision" is unchecked to eliminate Windows cursor acceleration.

3. Enable "Raw Input" in Game Settings

In games like Counter-Strike 2, Valorant, and Overwatch 2, enable Raw Input Buffer. This forces the game engine to read directly from the Windows HID API, bypassing OS-level cursor modifications.

4. Clean Sensor Lens & Mousepad

Sensor jitter is frequently caused by a single stray hair lodged across the optical prism. Blow compressed air directly into the sensor cavity and ensure your cloth mousepad is wiped free of dust and debris.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is angle snapping ever beneficial for anyone?

Yes, in graphic design and digital drafting. Digital artists and CAD engineers who don't have a graphics tablet occasionally enable angle snapping to draw perfectly straight horizontal or vertical lines without holding Shift.

How can I tell if my circle test failed?

When drawing freehand circles, look at the extreme top, bottom, left, and right points. A pure mouse sensor will produce smooth, continuous arcs. A mouse with angle snapping will generate flattened straight lines at the poles, making the circle look like an octagon or rounded diamond.

Does DPI affect angle snapping detection?

Higher DPI (such as 1600 or 3200 DPI) provides higher sample density, making our mathematical regression even more sensitive to artificial plateau runs. We recommend testing at your normal gaming DPI (800 or 1600 DPI).
