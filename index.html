Frequently Asked Questions

How do I test my mechanical keyboard online?

Open our Keyboard Tester and press any key. Each key lights up immediately upon activation, showing exact key codes and ghosting registration limits without installing drivers.

What causes mouse scroll jump or reverse scrolling?

Scroll jumping is usually caused by dust in the rotary optical or mechanical encoder, or worn contact spring leaves. You can test your mouse wheel ticks live on our Scroll Jump Tester.

Are these hardware tests 100% private and safe?

Yes, 100%. All diagnostics run directly in your local browser sandbox utilizing official W3C Web APIs. Zero keystrokes, audio, or video streams are ever stored or sent to remote servers.
